## Addendum 6 (Nov 15 2026): where the theory stands after reports 84-95
Frame: the vector is a rule-based pivot-ratchet state machine (no learned parameters; its Pine title says "SVM - Fractals" but the code is not a support vector machine) that classifies BULL/BEAR state with a confirmation lag. It labels state; it does not size the move. A flip pays only when the forward move beats the lag tax (~15% late entry, ~13% give-back on the big legs). Returns come from a rare tail (top 3 winners = 60-70% of some books; median flip loses ~5%), so the question is selecting moves larger than the lag.
Why it 'dies': (1) the 2019-21 universe mean was +4.6% per flip in a speculative boom with mean-reverting daily VR 0.7-0.9; 2022+ the whole-universe mean is ~0 (+0.2%), so with no tail selection there is nothing left; (2) the tail lives in low-priced / low-dollar-volume names (huge rate 3.5-16% under $5 vs 0.6-1.1% above $20), i.e. supply/attention, not institutions; (3) survivorship flatters the tail names; (4) confirmation lag is the price, not a bug (gap-to-next-open is ~0.0-0.4%).
What participant data said: 13F fund buys (15 funds incl. Renaissance) no pass; insider and catalyst proxies lumpy; volume at the flip bar is the one durable tilt; attention (movers) shows small continuation (+2.5 to +6.7pp/10d) but the vector adds nothing to it.
Leads (not yet traded): (a) stacked ridge-logistic flip score, frozen from 2019-21, AUC 0.563 all / 0.582 on $1-10, top-minus-bottom quintile +2.8pp / +9.2pp, positive every test year on $1-10; (b) isolated flips (few same-industry peers flipping first) beat crowded-theme flips (-2.5pp pooled t -2.2); (c) 1H flips catch moves earlier than daily (GEVO: 1H flip Oct 27 ~+57% marked vs daily flip ~flat) but 96% of 1H penny flips lose small; (d) 2y yield 60d change as the one fast regime driver; (e) squeeze regime filter (live). All survivorship-flattered, small-cap costs understated.
Dead ends (do not repeat): 13F/index/13D/biotech catalysts as gates, EMA/trend gates, open-entry on gap, hard stops, fade exits, bottom-cutting models, OHLCV move-size classifier (AUC 0.52-0.58), vector state as bias for other entries (report_84), vector level as a stop on movers (26-30% away), pullback-to-level entries, shorting bear flips or selected bear flips (report_92-94), ML bake-off (report_95): RBF SVM test AUC 0.484, gradient boosting 0.517 (train 0.72), random forest 0.553, logistic 0.563 best; nonlinear models overfit this feature set, so more model complexity is not the missing piece.
Missing: survivorship-free point-in-time prices (biggest), realistic small-cap costs/borrow, real-time participant data (options flow via Theta, shadow-score ledger on the Mac), more than 60 days of intraday history for 1H/5m penny work.
