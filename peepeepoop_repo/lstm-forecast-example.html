Target Probability
Price-only · Monte Carlo

What this is: simulates thousands of possible price paths from your entry and checks whether price reaches your target before your stop.

High vs Low: High means the simulation thinks price has the momentum to reach the target. Low means it doesn't. Counterintuitively, on 4h charts a Low reading on a candidate level has meant that level is more likely to hold (act as real support/resistance) rather than break — so Low is often the more useful reading for a reversion trade, not High.

What to look for: trust NVDA, TSLA, META. Stats for your selected ticker/timeframe show below once you run a simulation.
